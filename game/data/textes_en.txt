[histoire_titre]
Mob le Magus

[histoire]
Zorgus left you in charge of his dungeon. You tried a home-made spell. Bad idea.

You ran to your friend Isabela, who gave you a magic-eating potion.

Rush to the experiment room and fix it all before the master returns.

[spacio_magus_titre]
Spacio Magus

[spacio_magus]
The lit area shows how far your magic reaches.
Tap one of these tiles to teleport there!
Each spell costs one magic gem.

Tapping a neighbouring tile makes you walk there.
Tapping the magus skips your turn.

[spacio_creatura_titre]
Spacio Creatura

[spacio_creatura]
Tap a lit creature, then another one:

they swap places and stay stunned for the number of turns shown above them.

[porte_titre]
Doors

[porte]
Tap a neighbouring door to open it. The number tells how many turns until it shuts, pushing away whoever stands in it.

[grille_titre]
Portcullises

[grille]
It cannot be raised by hand: pull its lever from a neighbouring square. The number tells how many turns until it drops, pushing away whoever stands under it. Some portcullises have two levers: each stays pulled until the other one is, and both spring back when the portcullis drops.

[fiole_titre]
Potions

[fiole]
One sip and all your gems are full. Walk onto it to drink it. If you are already full, it will wait patiently.

[blob_vert_titre]
The green blob

[blob_vert]
Zorgus's favourite guard: soft, sticky and stubborn. If it touches you, it engulfs you and you will wait inside it for the master. Once it has seen you, it never lets go.

[blob_rouge_titre]
The red blob

[blob_rouge]
A green blob that got too scared of fire. Fire no longer stops it, but water terrifies it. Cornered by its fear, it turns green again.

[torche_titre]
The torch

[torche]
Green blobs fear fire: they never enter the three tiles below a torch, nor attack there. Blocked for too long, a green blob turns red.

[brasero_titre]
The brazier

[brasero]
A fire you cannot cross, but sight and magic pass through it. Green blobs keep away from the tiles around it.

[contenant_titre]
Crates, barrels and vases

[contenant]
You cannot teleport onto them. Tap one when you stand next to it to break it: it takes a turn. Sometimes a potion hides inside.

[escalier_titre]
The stairs

[escalier]
The way out of the level. Step on it to go deeper into the dungeon.

[eau_titre]
Water

[eau]
Mob devoted his life to magic and never found the time to learn to swim: you can neither walk on it nor teleport onto it, but sight and magic pass over it. Stuck with no gem, no potion and no container within reach, the game is lost.

[manette_titre]
The lever

[manette]
Tap it from a neighbouring square to raise the portcullis linked to it: it takes a turn. Impossible if a bat has landed on it. Some portcullises have two levers: each stays pulled until the other one is, and both spring back when the portcullis drops.

[alcove_titre]
The alcove

[alcove]
A niche lined with unlit candles. Light them from a neighbouring square: it takes a turn, and their glow makes the containers hiding a potion sparkle.

[chauve_souris_titre]
The bat

[chauve_souris]
It flies over water, containers and levers. Once it has spotted you, it follows you everywhere. Its bite steals a gem, then it dozes for two turns, sated. With no gem left, you faint.

[empale_titre]
Mob was impaled

[empale]
The spikes shot up under his feet. Mob will wait for Zorgus to return… without being able to sit down.

[parchemin_pris_titre]
Improved Spacio Creatura

[parchemin_pris]
The scroll holds an improved version of Spacio Creatura: the spell now also swaps big objects (barrels, crates, vases and chests), in any mix with creatures.

This power only lasts for this level.

[fin_demo_titre]
End of the demo

[fin_demo]
Well done, Mob made it through the three rooms of this demo!

If the demo finds its audience, a full version with ten levels will be made. Thank you for playing.

[bouton_recommencer_demo]
Restart the demo

[coffre_titre]
Chests

[coffre]
They overflow with gold, but gold is useless to a magus. Tap one from a neighbouring square to open or close it: it takes a turn. They cannot be broken or teleported onto.

[cle_titre]
The key

[cle]
Walk onto it to pick it up. It opens the locked door, once only: after that, the door stays unlocked.

[porte_verrouillee_titre]
The locked door

[porte_verrouillee]
It only opens with the key. Once unlocked, it behaves like a normal door.

[piege_titre]
Spike traps

[piege]
The spikes stay down for three turns, then come up for one turn. When they are up, you can neither walk nor teleport onto them, and if they come up under the magus, it is over. They stun blobs; bats do not care.

[parchemin_titre]
The altar and the scroll

[parchemin]
The altar cannot be crossed. Tap the scroll from a neighbouring square to take it: it takes a turn, and it improves Spacio Creatura for this level.

[autel_vide_titre]
The empty altar

[autel_vide]
The altar cannot be crossed. The scroll has already been taken.

[geste_titre]
Long press

[geste]
To learn everything about a door, a creature, an item or the magus himself, keep your finger on it.

On a computer, just right-click.
