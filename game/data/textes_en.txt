[histoire_titre]
Spacio Magus

[histoire]
Zorgus left you in charge of his dungeon. You tried a home-made spell. Bad idea.

You ran to your friend Isabela, who gave you a magic-eating potion. Back home, the dungeon had locked itself down.

Rush to the experiment room and fix it all before the master returns.

[spacio_magus_titre]
Spacio Magus

[spacio_magus]
Your magic moves things. It never hurts.

Tap a free lit tile to teleport there. The lit area shows how far your magic reaches.

Each spell costs one gem. Tap the magus to rest and get one back.

[spacio_creatura_titre]
Spacio Creatura

[spacio_creatura]
Tap a lit creature, then another one: they swap places and stay stunned. The number above them counts the turns left.

To walk, tap a neighbouring tile.

[porte_titre]
Doors

[porte]
Tap a neighbouring door to open it. The number tells how many turns until it shuts, pushing away whoever stands in it. A closed door blocks sight and magic.

[fiole_titre]
Potions

[fiole]
One sip and all your gems are full. Walk onto it to drink it. If you are already full, it will wait patiently.

[blob_vert_titre]
The green blob

[blob_vert]
Zorgus's favourite guard: soft, sticky and stubborn. If it touches you, it engulfs you and you will wait inside it for the master. Once it has seen you, it never lets go.
